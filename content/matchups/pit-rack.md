### Pit Rack

{cards: The Rack | Bottomless Pit | Funeral Charm | Chain of Smog}

{cards: Cursed Scroll | Pox}

**Verdict.** Even.

**What they do.** A mono-black deck that shares a lot of its cards and its plan with Moneyball Black (read [Moneyball Black](#moneyball-black) first), with fewer creatures and more ways to attack your hand: Funeral Charm, Chain of Smog, and Bottomless Pit, which makes each player discard a card at random every upkeep.

Their win condition is The Rack, which punishes you for a low hand count: at the beginning of your upkeep it deals one damage for each card you hold below three. They also play Cursed Scroll, since they empty their own hand quite fast, and some lists register Pox as well, which is also a removal spell: it makes each player sacrifice a third of their creatures, rounded up, so a lone Dreadnought goes.

**Game one.** Use Gush, and Flash of Insight's flashback, to bring your hand back up to three — cast from your hand, Flash only replaces itself. Both are instants, and The Rack counts your hand when its trigger resolves, so you can do it in response to the trigger. When they Chain of Smog you, you may copy it and point the copy back at them — but not while you still have cards in hand. Whoever is targeted can copy it again, so the copies go back and forth until both players have no cards, and an empty hand on both sides is exactly what the Pit Rack player wants.

**Sideboarding:** −4 Foil, −1 Brain Freeze, −1 Peek / +3 Annul, +2 Back to Basics, +1 Flare.

**Why these.** Their engines are permanents, so all three Annuls come in: counter The Rack and Bottomless Pit on cast. Both Back to Basics come in for Mishra's Factory, as against Moneyball Black; their Wastelands, and the Rishadan Ports some lists add, suffer too. One Flare is enough for Specter and Plaguebearer in a deck with fewer creatures. All four Foils leave: The Rack punishes every card below three in your hand, and Bottomless Pit takes one every upkeep, so a counter that costs two cards works against you even late. Brain Freeze and Peek leave for the reasons they leave against Moneyball Black.
