### Blue-White Web Control

{cards: Akroma's Vengeance | Tsabo's Web | Decree of Justice | Eternal Dragon}

**Verdict.** Even.

**What they do.** Very similar to Landstill in construction and philosophy (read [Landstill](#landstill) first), but without manlands and without Standstill. That makes its mana much more consistent and hard to attack — consistent enough to play Tsabo's Web in the main deck or the sideboard, which gives the deck its name. It plays full sweepers in Akroma's Vengeance, and tries to win off Decree of Justice alone; some versions add a couple of top-heavy threats in the main, Eternal Dragon or Exalted Angel.

The plan is the Landstill one; only the sideboarding changes.

**Sideboarding:** −1 Dreadnought, −1 Stifle, −2 Daze, −1 Foil / +3 Flare, +2 Brain Freeze.

**Why these.** No Back to Basics: there are no manlands to stop, and their mana is too consistent for it to matter. No Annul either, with no Standstill and no Humility to aim it at. The third Flare comes in instead, for Meddling Mage as against Landstill, and an extra Dreadnought, an extra Foil and the Peek stay in.
