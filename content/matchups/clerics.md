### Mono-Black Clerics

{cards: Rotlung Reanimator | Dark Supplicant | Scion of Darkness | Cabal Archon}

{cards: Priest of Gix | Recurring Nightmare | Phyrexian Tower}

**Verdict.** Slightly unfavorable.

**What they do.** A mono-black strategy like Moneyball Black, and the matchup plays out in a similar way, so read [Moneyball Black](#moneyball-black) first: they have the full discard suite, and they attack your hand the same way.

They are also a tribal creature deck with two value engines. The first, and the most threatening, is Rotlung Reanimator: every time it or another Cleric dies, they get a 2/2 Zombie. Once it is on the battlefield, the deck has plenty of ways to sacrifice its own creatures — Cabal Archon, Phyrexian Tower, Cabal Therapy's flashback, Recurring Nightmare and Dark Supplicant — and those quickly snowball into a large board of Zombies.

The second engine is Dark Supplicant. Untap with it and enough Clerics around it, and it sacrifices three of them to find Scion of Darkness, a 6/6 trampler and a fast clock.

Priest of Gix and Recurring Nightmare can also loop: the three mana from the Priest entering the battlefield pays for Recurring Nightmare again, so watch out for that combination.

**What the matchup hinges on.** The engines. Your highest priority is to counter Rotlung Reanimator and to Stifle Dark Supplicant's activation — Stifle it after the three Clerics are sacrificed as the cost, and they have given up three creatures for nothing. They don't play much removal, so jamming a Dreadnought early wins a lot of games.

**Sideboarding:** −2 Foil, −1 Brain Freeze, −1 Peek / +1 Chain, +3 Flare.

**Why these.** The Moneyball Black plan, all three Flares included — their creatures are what you sideboard for — with one difference: Back to Basics stays in the sideboard, since their mana is Swamps, so two Foils stay in.
