### Moneyball Black

{cards: Hypnotic Specter | Ravenous Rats | Cabal Therapy | Duress}

{cards: Dark Ritual | Graveborn Muse | Nantuko Shade | Withered Wretch}

{cards: Smother | Cursed Scroll | Mishra's Factory}

**Verdict.** Slightly unfavorable. They attack the one resource the combo can't do without — cards in hand — and they do it from the first turn.

**What they do.** Mono-black discard, built to empty your hand before you can assemble the combo. Four Duress, four Cabal Therapy, four Ravenous Rats and four Hypnotic Specter. Rats and Therapy are a combo of their own: Rats makes you discard a card (your choice), then Therapy names one and flashes back by sacrificing the Rats to name another, three cards from your hand for two of theirs. Specter, left alone, takes a random card every time it connects. Four Dark Ritual power out the explosive starts: Ritual into Specter on the first turn, Ritual into Phyrexian Arena in the lists that run it, or two Rituals into Graveborn Muse.

Around the discard: pump creatures that use their mana every turn, Nantuko Shade and Knight of Stromgald, and Withered Wretch, a beater that can also exile your graveyard — which makes your Flash of Insight worse. The removal is Smother, Vendetta and Snuff Out, with Diabolic Edict or Chainer's Edict beside them, and many lists play Plaguebearer in the main deck — one of the most annoying anti-Dreadnought cards you can face: for three mana it kills your Dreadnought, and it does so again every turn. They grind with Cursed Scroll and with four Mishra's Factory, and play Wasteland alongside them.

**What the matchup hinges on.** The fight over cards. Their deck usually empties its hand within the first couple of turns, so the game becomes cards in hand against cards in hand, and every trade you make has to be one for one or better. Whatever they are still holding after that is most likely removal you need to account for. And after both hands are traded off, the Factories are the clock that finishes you.

**Mulligans.** As [Mulligans](#mulligans) says for every discard deck, you can't mulligan aggressively here: keep nearly any seven with lands and spells, because every card is a card they have to strip. Cabal Therapy cast blind after seeing an Island will very often name Phyrexian Dreadnought, so a hand with more than one Dreadnought is a risky keep — one Therapy takes both.

**Game one.** Fight over the cards, and don't hand them two-for-ones.

Daze is at its best against the Ritual starts, when they have tapped out to put a threat down on the first turn.

It is often correct to Counterspell a Duress or a Cabal Therapy: a Therapy when you hold the Dreadnought, either of them when they already know what to name or take. The discard would likely take that Counterspell anyway, or a better card, and by countering it you also hide information that would make their next Therapy better. Remember that Therapy comes twice, and Mishra's Factory gives them the creature to sacrifice: the Factory taps for the mana to animate itself, then goes to pay the flashback. Say a Duress shows them two Dreadnoughts and a Counterspell: with Therapy and a Factory they cast Therapy naming the Dreadnought, and when you counter it, they flash it back off the Factory and take both anyway. When Gush is the best card in your hand, Gush in response to a Duress or a Therapy: you draw two before the discard resolves, and at least go up a card.

Flash of Insight is one of your best cards here: the copy in your graveyard is a card they can't strip, there when you need it. Only Withered Wretch threatens it, by exiling it or the blue cards you would remove to flash it back. Duress can't take the Dreadnought — it's a creature — but Therapy can, and so can a Specter hit; Rats makes you discard, but you choose what.

This is a great matchup to Portent the opponent when you're ahead. Once their hand is empty they are living off the top of their library, so setting their next three draws — lands on top, the Specter or the removal at the bottom, or a shuffle when all three are good — can strand them for turns while you build your commit.

Withered Wretch, Nantuko Shade and Knight of Stromgald are quick clocks, and they will force you to jam earlier than you want to. Charm the Dreadnought in response to Smother, Snuff Out or Vendetta (a Vendetta that resolves on the Dreadnought costs them twelve life); against an Edict, Charm in response leaves you no creature to sacrifice.

Stifle's targets all have tempo value. A Mishra's Factory activation. A Graveborn Muse or Phyrexian Arena trigger, to keep a card from them when they are digging for an answer. A Specter trigger, when the card at risk is worth more than the Stifle. A Cursed Scroll activation, to stay alive and win the race.

Powder Keg at two kills Rats, Shade, Knight, Wretch and Plaguebearer without touching your Dreadnought; at zero, an animated Factory. Cursed Scroll needs Keg at one, the Dreadnought's own setting, so Charm the Dreadnought first.

**What to counter.** Specter on sight. An early Graveborn Muse or Phyrexian Arena, before the extra cards start. Therapy when it would hit the Dreadnought, and Duress or Therapy when they already know your hand. Their removal, when the Dreadnought is in play and Charm isn't there.

**After sideboarding.** They bring more removal — Diabolic Edict, Smother, Snuff Out — and more Plaguebearers. Many bring Tormod's Crypt, which empties your graveyard of the Flash of Insight you were counting on. Some bring Powder Keg, which at one kills the Dreadnought on its own: Charm the Dreadnought in response when they crack it.

{cards: Plaguebearer | Powder Keg | Diabolic Edict | Tormod's Crypt}

You bring Essence Flare as an extra answer to Specter, Plaguebearer and Withered Wretch. A second Chain of Vapor sends back whatever they Dark Ritualed in, so the Ritual is a card spent for nothing. Back to Basics cuts their mana — the Factories and Wastelands stop untapping — and keeps a Factory that has attacked tapped for good; mono-black has little that can remove it.

**Sideboarding:** −4 Foil, −1 Brain Freeze, −1 Peek / +2 Back to Basics, +1 Chain, +3 Flare.

**Why these.** Foil is your worst card here: your hand is under attack, and you often can't afford to pitch a card to cast it. Peek goes because they empty their hands so quickly that there is often nothing much to look at. Brain Freeze goes because you aren't on the Freeze plan here. Flare's targets are Plaguebearer and Knight, which die in one upkeep, and Specter, which takes two — though they can answer the Flare trigger on Plaguebearer by activating it at your Dreadnought, so have Charm or Stifle behind it. Wretch is a target too, but not one you need to answer on sight: Flare it when its clock is adding up quickly, remembering that the extra power hits you for the two turns it takes.

Daze stays for the Ritual starts. Keg stays: at two it kills five of their creatures, at three Specter, at four the Muse. Flash stays, because it is the card advantage they can't discard, though Wretch can exile it. Thwart stays: it is the free counter you need once your hand has been taken apart.
