## Resources

Many of the ideas and concepts in this primer were inspired by the resources below, and every one of them is recommended reading.

**Primers and guides for the decks you will face**

Some of these aren't public, and some are paid. For those, ask the authors on the deck's Discord: [Premodern Hub](https://www.premodernhub.com/) links to every Premodern Discord and to the public resources.

- [The hFEB guide](https://hfeb.guide/) — Full English Breakfast, from the inside. Excellent.
- The Xerox Tog primer, by Robert Luemers — Psychatog (in the Resources section of the [Psychatog Discord](https://discord.gg/gNYfdUyW5R)).
- The definitive guide to playing Stasis in Premodern, by Rodrigo Hartl — Stasis (ask on the [Stasis Discord](https://discord.gg/jmbHsBcHZx)).
- [The Replenish primer](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vTxOv1l0Fqfj3okC-MZxulcRHIkn5cOK6JTrtnNE7Q4_hgQe3g1C2FOlGwTb3X_rmjccZ74tNRWaxIX/pub), by Dr. Suiss — Replenish.
- [The Naya Terrageddon Ultimate Guide](https://metafy.gg/guides/view/naya-terrageddon-ultimate-guide-EZ9kc6oiKh4), by Sam White — Terrageddon (paid, on Metafy).
- The Burn Ward matchup and sideboard guide — Sligh and Burn (ask on the [Burn Discord](https://discord.gg/Yxd36mCWh9)).
- The Premodern Goblins primer, by golgariguy — Goblins (ask on the [Goblins Discord](https://discord.gg/uj6TXtwVXN)).
- The Survival Madness primer, by Cyberpunker — Madness (ask on the [Survival Madness Discord](https://discord.gg/p4tbZnHR3K)).

**For the Dreadnought**

- Dave Kaplan's lists, his Lobstercon lists in particular — the pedigree for the jam build and for this sideboard's Essence Flare and Back to Basics.
- [The Blue Shrimp primer](https://docs.google.com/document/d/100BCtTRMn5pj4VT4Hmv0E2X-1Pke44RKbMyU7R8WXoY/), by Dvraska — the deck's bible in the Parallax Tide era.
- [Mastering Stiflenought in Premodern](https://ultimateguard.com/en/blog/mastering-stiflenought-in-premodern-deck-guide-strategy-sideboard-plans), on the Ultimate Guard blog — a deck guide with sideboard plans.
- [Lanny's quick-start video on the deck](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YsjtzKc_jDk), from the Tide era; its ideas on casting cantrips first, the two-swing clock and answers on the stack shaped How the deck plays.

**Help improve it.** Spotted a mistake, disagree with a sideboard plan, or have a matchup to add? Tell me on the Shrimp, [open an issue](https://github.com/mlongob/dreadnought-primer/issues/new), or [send a pull request](https://github.com/mlongob/dreadnought-primer/pulls): the whole primer is plain text in a public repository.

**Where the numbers come from.** The numbers in this primer come from my tournament database: 16,796 decklists from January 18 to October 6, 2026 — MTGO Challenge and League feeds, mtgtop8 and mtgdecks, with paper events merged across sources — and 8,112 from 2025, with win-loss records where standings exist. Every share, conversion, win rate, card frequency and stock list in these pages comes from it. It stores placements, not pairings, so there are no per-matchup win rates.
