## What's changed

This primer is a living document: it changes as the format does and as I learn more about the deck. Each version is listed here, newest first. To suggest a change, [open an issue](https://github.com/mlongob/dreadnought-primer/issues/new) or [send a pull request](https://github.com/mlongob/dreadnought-primer/pulls), or find me on the Shrimp.

**Version 0.1 — October 2026.** The first public version.

- The deck: what it is and where it stands, its history and versions, the 75, how it plays, every card in it, the mana base, the sideboard and how to use it, every answer to the Dreadnought, and mulligans.
- Matchups: a guide for most of the decks you will face, and a quick-reference sideboard map. The ones marked coming soon show their sideboard plan where there is one; their guides are on the way.
- Practice: puzzles to work through, each with its answer behind a spoiler.
- Resources: the primers, creators and communities behind these pages.
