## What's changed

This primer is a living document: it changes as the format does and as I learn more about the deck. Each version is listed here, newest first. To suggest a change, [open an issue](https://github.com/mlongob/dreadnought-primer/issues/new) or [send a pull request](https://github.com/mlongob/dreadnought-primer/pulls), or find me on the Shrimp.

**Version 0.4 — November 1, 2026.** Changes from readers' feedback; the people behind them are thanked in [Resources](#resources).

- Fixes: Powder Keg's settings (Negator is mana value 3), why a phased-in Dreadnought can attack, Spike Feeder doesn't answer the Dreadnought, Fire // Ice and Mana Short, a Chain on the Dreadnought always costs you the enabler, Stifling a single Port gains nothing, Misdirection can send a counter onto the Misdirection, why the blue Blasts stay out against GR Oath Ponza, and the cantrip counts and wording in Mulligans.
- When the Freeze plan is right: decks with many answers and few threats, or decks that build the storm for you; and building the storm by countering a single spell of theirs several times.
- Annul is sized by how much its targets matter as well as how many there are.
- Goblin Vandal in Sligh and in Answers to the Dreadnought, and the Welder risk of a cracked Keg.

**Version 0.3 — October 19, 2026.** Changes from readers' feedback; the people behind them are thanked in [Resources](#resources).

- Close candidates: the cards that aren't in this list are presented as choices, and Boomerang is compared with the Chain it would replace.
- Foil: when a pitched Foil is worth three cards, and why Sylvan Library isn't, repeated in Terrageddon and Oath Ponza.
- Sligh: Peek stays after sideboarding and an Opt goes instead.
- The Psychatog entry is now UB Psychatog, with when to fight over Duress; Grow-a-Tog now covers the other Tog decks, Esper Tog and the main-deck Mage lists included. UB Psychatog's verdict is now favorable.

**Version 0.2 — October 14, 2026.** Changes from readers' feedback; the people behind them are thanked in [Resources](#resources).

- The mirror: the second Chain comes in for the Keg after sideboarding, and Thwart stays in place of Peek.
- GW Oath Ponza now covers the five-color versions too, with Glowrider, three Flares, and a slower game after sideboarding.
- Moneyball Black: a Dreadnought and a Stifle come out after sideboarding, and only two Foils.
- The main-deck Brain Freeze is the flex slot, with Misdirection, a second Keg or a third Flash as the alternatives, and Misdirection gets the weight it deserves.
- Close candidates: Prohibit, Morphling, Masticore and Mage's Guile, and a third Flash for the sideboard.
- The first puzzle lists doing nothing as an option.
- Fixes: Foil can pitch non-blue cards, and Spock and Luffy are Brazilian.

**Version 0.1 — October 12, 2026.** The first public version.

- The deck: what it is and where it stands, its history and versions, the 75, how it plays, every card in it, the mana base, the sideboard and how to use it, every answer to the Dreadnought, and mulligans.
- Matchups: a guide for most of the decks you will face, and a quick-reference sideboard map. The ones marked coming soon show their sideboard plan where there is one; their guides are on the way.
- Practice: puzzles to work through, each with its answer behind a spoiler.
- Resources: the primers, creators and communities behind these pages.
