# Roadmap

Living list of what's next for the primer. Newest decisions first within each section.

## Planned (the author's list, October 2026)

2. **Resources and videos** — creators who play the deck (Lanny, Neon Mushroom, Grindstone Cowboy, …),
   notable matches from big paper tournaments, communities to join. The author supplies the links.
   Links so far: the "three gears" quick-start video, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YsjtzKc_jDk (Tide era;
   its ideas on cantrips-first, the two-swing clock and answers on the stack informed How the deck plays).
3. **About the author** — the author's bio, and a thank-you to the people who helped review the content and
   to their Magic community.
4. **Practice puzzles** — board states where the reader decides the play, applying what the primer teaches
   by deduction. The rendering and the Practice chapter are built (2026-10-11: MTGO-style boards, phase bar,
   hidden and seen cards, land stacks, phasing, spoiler answers); the puzzles themselves come from the author.
   Puzzle 1 (two Dreadnoughts against Sligh) is the first real one. The stack (spells and abilities, leaning toward whoever put them there, targets as a note) and an optional storm count are built too.
5. **Credits** — the older primers and the other primers the author draws inspiration from (lifts the
   current "no credits in the text" rule once this section exists).

## Also proposed, not yet scheduled


- **How the deck plays, expanded** — enabler choice, playing around answers, cantrip timing, three or four
  games narrated turn by turn. The biggest gap the review found. Needs the author's dictation.
- **Common mistakes / the commit checklist** — from the corrections list and the review (forgetting
  Portent's draw in paper, Charming the Dreadnought under Stasis, Gushing into discards, …).
- **Rules FAQ** — the rulings scattered through the card entries, as short questions and answers.
- **Paper versus MTGO** — why the deck is dominant online and good-not-dominant in paper. Needs the author.
- **Practice path** — how a new pilot should learn the deck. Needs the author.
- **Matchups** — a must-counter line and who's the beatdown for each; the remaining matchups rewritten to
  the full template at roughly Goblins' length: the ones marked coming soon (Zoo and the rest), and Survival.
- **Trim** — the Dreadnought-answers census as a table or reorganized by our response; card-analysis
  enumerations into tables; the 24 example hands cut to about 12 grouped by lesson.
